# FeedFix

## Problem and user
A Walmart Marketplace seller has a rejected item setup workbook and needs a concrete diagnosis and a safe correction, immediately.

## Offer and willingness to pay
Upload a file, see useful errors free, pay $4.99 once for deterministic corrections and an auditable change report. Saves spreadsheet troubleshooting time without granting account access.

## Alternatives and differentiation
Manual Excel edits, Walmart processing reports and Seller Support are the immediate alternatives. Catalog management platforms are a broader category of competition; no unverified claims about competitors. FeedFix sells one narrow outcome, without accounts or subscriptions. Never invent identifiers or catalog facts.

## Risks
Official templates vary by category/version; sample schemas do not prove compatibility. Cosmetic corrections may not resolve rejection. Identity changes can cause harm. Sensitive data needs short retention. Buyers may prefer manual edits after free diagnosis. Measure paid downloads before adding scope.

## Success
Revenue > $0 from a user we don't know.
A tested funnel is engineering readiness, not commercial validation. Launch to real sellers only after validating at least one official template and its processing report; fixture mode cannot accept live payments.
